# 🎮 MindMapFlow AI - Demo Guide

Welcome to the MindMapFlow AI Demo! This guide will help you quickly experience all the amazing features of our AI-powered visualization tool.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Launch the App
- The app should be running at `http://localhost:3000`
- You'll see the beautiful MindMapFlow AI interface with the brain and flowchart icons

### 2. First Look
- Notice the clean, modern interface with the gradient logo
- Toggle between light/dark mode using the moon/sun icon in the top-right
- Select your AI provider (Gemini AI or OpenAI GPT-4) from the dropdown

## 🧠 Demo 1: Mind Map Generation

### Try the Sample Content
1. Click **"Sample: Mind Map"** button to load sample content about Photosynthesis
2. Notice how the content is structured with:
   - Main topic definition
   - Key components and processes
   - Real-world applications

### Generate Your First Mind Map
1. Click **"Generate Mind Map"** (purple button)
2. Watch the progress indicator show:
   - "Analyzing your notes..."
   - "Creating mind map structure..."
   - "Complete!"

### Explore the Mind Map
- **Central Node**: Main topic (Photosynthesis) in the center
- **Subtopic Nodes**: Color-coded branches radiating outward
- **Detail Nodes**: Smaller nodes with specific points
- **Interactive Features**:
  - Zoom in/out with the controls
  - Pan by dragging
  - Hover over nodes for tooltips

### Export Your Mind Map
- Click the **Download** button (green) for PNG export
- Click **"PDF"** button for high-quality PDF export
- Files are automatically named based on your topic

## 📊 Demo 2: Flowchart Generation

### Load Process-Based Content
1. Click **"Sample: Flowchart"** to load "How to Solve a Math Problem"
2. Notice the step-by-step, procedural nature of the content

### Generate Your First Flowchart
1. Click **"Generate Flowchart"** (blue button)
2. Watch the AI analyze and structure the process

### Explore the Flowchart
- **Start Node** (Green): Beginning of the process
- **Process Nodes** (Blue): Action steps
- **Decision Nodes** (Yellow): Decision points/branches  
- **End Node** (Red): Process completion
- **Interactive Features**:
  - Drag nodes to reposition them
  - Use minimap for navigation
  - Zoom and pan with built-in controls
